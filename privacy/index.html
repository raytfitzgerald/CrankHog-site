Privacy policy

Your app data stays on your device.

Updated 15 October 2026. Covers the Mac app, the iPhone app, the web app and this website.

What stays on your device

Your rides, workouts, routes, journeys, FTP history, profile (name, photo, weight, heart-rate zones) and settings are stored only on your Mac, iPhone or browser. Every ride is also saved as a FIT file on the same device. The CrankHog apps have no accounts, no servers that hold your ride data, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Website download counts are described separately below.

Bluetooth

CrankHog uses Bluetooth only to connect to your smart trainer, heart-rate strap and other cycling sensors, so it can set the resistance and record your ride. Nothing about your devices leaves your device.

Things you choose to connect

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you set it up yourself:

  • Strava or intervals.icu (Mac app): when you connect your account, finished rides are uploaded to it. Your keys and tokens are kept in your Mac's keychain.
  • AI coaching (Mac app): if you add an API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI or a local Ollama, the rides you ask about are sent to that provider to answer you. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Bug reports: the report form opens a GitHub issue in your browser with the details you can see and edit. Nothing is sent unless you submit it on GitHub.

Maps

Journey maps are drawn from road data that ships inside the app (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Riding a journey doesn't contact any map service. If you turn on "Map on Strava", the virtual route is written into that ride's FIT file and uploaded with it.

Children

CrankHog isn't aimed at children. The apps collect no data from anyone, of any age.

Website download counts

This website uses GoatCounter to count clicks on the Mac download buttons. These are download attempts, not completed downloads or installs. This tracking does not run inside the apps.

Every recorded click is counted, including repeat clicks. This is not a count of unique people. GoatCounter processes the IP address and browser User-Agent that accompany network requests. It does not store raw IP addresses or full User-Agent strings in its database.

No visitor identifier is saved in cookies or browser storage. This integration sends fixed event names rather than page query strings or referring URLs, and does not send automatic pageviews. Optional browser, device, language and location statistics, visitor sessions, and individual pageview storage are disabled in our GoatCounter settings. Visitors with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track enabled are skipped.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
